== What you will need

To complete this guide, you will need the following:

* Some time on your hands
* A decent text editor or IDE (e.g. https://micronaut-projects.github.io/micronaut-guides/latest/micronaut-intellij-idea-ide-setup.html[IntelliJ IDEA])
* JDK 25 or greater installed with `JAVA_HOME` configured appropriately
* https://www.docker.io/gettingstarted/#h_installation[Docker].
