Acme Space Travel cancellation policy

Passengers who cancel a flight to the Moon more than 30 days before launch get a full refund.
Passengers who cancel between 30 and 7 days before launch get a 50 percent refund.
Cancellations less than 7 days before launch are not refunded, because the fuel has already been loaded.
If Acme Space Travel postpones a launch because of a solar storm, passengers can rebook for free.
